Submissions

We are looking mainly for non-fiction writing. Works of fiction or poetry will be considered if they tell the truth.

Submissions may be as short or long as you choose.

You do not need to be a writer to submit, nor do you have to have written in the past. You may publish by name or by pseudonym.

Submit your writing to abotwinfo@gmail.com.
